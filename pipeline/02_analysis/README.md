# 02_analysis

Analyses answering the research questions.

- `01_thermophilization/`: community climatic-affinity indices (CTI, CMI), their rates of change and lags, per layer (`01_overstory`, `02_understory`) and for both layers together (`03_integration_over_under`).
- `02_hmsc/`: hierarchical joint species distribution models (HMSC) of species change between surveys, with traits and phylogeny.
