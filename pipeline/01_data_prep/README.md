# 01_data_prep

Cleaning and preparation of the inputs of the analyses.

- `01_cleaning/`: plot identifiers, species names, present-day census selection, overstory and understory tables.
- `02_species_pool/`: species pools and their trait and affinity coverage.
- `03_climate/`: CHELSA monthly climate, past and present climate windows.
- `04_traits/`: species traits (TRY, GIFT, BIEN, literature), imputation and phylogeny.
- `05_species_distribution/`: GBIF occurrences and species climatic affinities.
- `06_exploring/`: exploratory summaries of the cleaned data.
